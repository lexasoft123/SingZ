# Instrument samples

Piano, electric piano 1, and nylon guitar: FluidR3 GM by Frank Wen and S. Christian Collins, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.
Source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

C2–C6 MP3 samples extracted from the pre-rendered instrument files. Modified for training: per-note level adjustment, 4:1 compression (8 ms attack, 120 ms release), makeup gain and peak limiting. Playback transposes samples at runtime.
