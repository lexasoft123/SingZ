Fixes for the new player. Changing training, the click or the count-in while it counts you in mid-song no longer throws the song back to the top. If headphones, a car or a speaker took over the sound between opening a song and pressing Play, Play now follows it instead of doing nothing until you reopened the song. On the computer, a song with no beat grid yet plays with the click on, and a part you marked without repeat stops at its end on every Play, not just the first.
