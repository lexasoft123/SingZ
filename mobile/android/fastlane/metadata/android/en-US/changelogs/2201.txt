Fixes for the new player. On the computer, a song with no beat grid yet plays with the click on instead of refusing to start, and a part you marked without repeat stops at its end on every Play, not just the first. On your phone, changing training, the click or the count-in while it counts you in mid-song no longer throws the song back to the top, and Play after a pause with the count-in on no longer flashes 0:00 first.
