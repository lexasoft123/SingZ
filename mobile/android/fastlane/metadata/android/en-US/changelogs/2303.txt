MP3 songs you haven't split yet — and MP3 tracks you've added to a song — now play on SingZ's own audio engine, read a little at a time as they play instead of being unpacked whole into memory first. A six-minute MP3 used to cost about 140 MB of memory just to open. On the computer, a Windows laptop that slept with a song paused, or lost its audio device another way, no longer leaves every song silent until you restart SingZ.
