A-B repeat lands on the phone: mark where the hard bar starts, mark where it ends, and it goes round until you have it. Songs on the phone itself get a swipe: Detect again, which is new here, plus Find lyrics and Delete, which used to hide behind an unannounced long-press. The mixer answers Full mix / No vocals / Vocals only in one tap. Every control can say its own name to a screen reader, the Add-a-song sheet no longer hides under the keyboard, and the player has a new dusk-lit stage.
