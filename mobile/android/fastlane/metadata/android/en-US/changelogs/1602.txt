Nothing in the app changed in this one — it is all packaging. SingZ was carrying a second and third copy of its whole audio engine, built for emulator chips no phone has: 121 MB of dead weight in a 234 MB package, gone. On an Intel Chromebook SingZ now runs through ARM translation — still installable, just slower. Play only ever sent your phone its own share, so your download barely moves; it is the APK on the Releases page that halves.
