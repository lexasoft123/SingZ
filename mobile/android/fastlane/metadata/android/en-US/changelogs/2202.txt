One fix, on the phone. The sample song that comes with SingZ stopped the moment you left the app or locked the phone. It always plays on the older audio engine — it is unpacked from inside the app instead of being read from files — and leaving the app silenced that engine whatever it was doing, so any song playing on it went quiet too. Now a song the phone agreed to keep playing carries on behind the Home Screen, and the Lock Screen's play, pause and scrub bar reach it while it does.
