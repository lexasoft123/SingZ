v0.12.0 — the words light up as you sing them

Karaoke stopped snapping and started sweeping, the library is one click away, and the app finally has its own volume knob.

## The words light up as you sing them

Until now a word flipped from unsung to sung the instant it started — one hard switch per word, which is not how singing feels. Now the colour travels *through* the word as you sing it, with a soft edge that moves with the voice. The word ahead stays unlit, so you can still read ahead of yourself.

Between words the app knows the difference between the aligner being vague and you taking a breath: a gap under a third of a second is slack, so the fill glides through it; anything longer is a real breath and the word simply stays lit until the next one starts.

The phones do the same sweep, and it moves at the display's rate rather than React's — on a mid-range Android that cut dropped frames from ~110 to ~38 over twelve seconds.

## Your library, without closing the song

The library used to be reachable only through the "Open…" dialog, or by closing the song entirely. There is now a **Catalog** button next to the logo: it swaps in the full library page — the same grid the app opens on, with search, cards and Drive badges — while your song stays loaded behind it. Esc, or a second click, and you are back exactly where you were: karaoke, lanes, playhead, all untouched.

Opening the catalog pauses playback, on purpose — the transport goes away with the song view, and nothing should be left playing with no way to stop it.

## A volume knob of its own

Six stem faders, and no way to turn the whole thing down without reaching for the system volume. There is now a speaker button in the bottom panel with a master slider and a mute that remembers where it came back from. Arrow keys move the focused slider instead of seeking the song.

The metronome bypasses the master *bus* (so tempo and pitch correction never colour the click) but not the master *volume* — pulling everything down takes the click with it, as it should. The level lives with your audio preferences, next to the device pickers, not inside a project.

## The count-in keeps getting smarter

- In a passage the beat model repaired, the "1" is now voted by the **harmony** rather than counted blindly forward from the edges. On a song with a long bass solo, the accents now land on the chord changes where the ear puts them.
- One song drifted between 0:33 and 1:16 for a maddening reason: the same code produced a 117.8 bpm grid in testing and 58.9 bpm in the app, because two audio decoders read the same file a hair differently and the half-time/double-time race was decided by that hair. Near-ties are now settled by how the beats actually alternate — which survives any decoder.
- The full detection story, including every trap paid for along the way, is written down in `docs/BEAT-DETECTION.md` for the curious.

## Small joys

- Turning the microphone off now actually stops the pitch strip repainting. One "Match my singing" session used to leave it redrawing the whole canvas every frame — drawing nothing — until you closed the song. Laptop fans everywhere say thank you.

**No new pack this time:** the splitter and beat model are unchanged since v0.11.x, so nothing to download — the app updates and gets on with it.
