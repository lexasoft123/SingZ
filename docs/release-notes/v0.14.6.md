## The icon takes your launcher's shape

Android has let launchers choose the outline of an app icon since 2017 — circle,
squircle, rounded square, whatever the phone's theme prefers. SingZ was not
playing along, so it arrived as a square sitting inside a white badge, looking
like something sideloaded in a hurry. It now ships as an adaptive icon: the six
lanes on the app's own dark ground, inset far enough that no mask can clip them.
The background is the artwork's exact edge colour, so there is no seam to spot.

## Signing in to Drive stopped shouting its own secret

The app protects its sign-in with PKCE, which works by keeping one secret to
itself and proving later that it knew it. Until now it sent that secret along
with the request that was supposed to protect it — a locked box with the key
taped to the lid — and generated it from the clock plus a random number
generator that is not built for keeping secrets.

The secret now comes from the phone's cryptographic generator, and what travels
is a SHA-256 of it. There is nothing to see and nothing to do; the door was
always narrow, and it is now also shut properly.

## Android 7 was let go, and not for the reason you would guess

Not device counts — nine years on, there are barely any. The reason is that the
audio engine ships two ways of talking to the speaker and picks by Android
version: the modern one from Android 8, an older one below it. Every test this
app has ever had ran on the modern path. The other was code we shipped, never
ran, and could not vouch for.

For an app whose whole job is playing sound, supporting a sound path nobody has
heard is not support. It is a guess with a version number on it. So it is gone,
and every phone SingZ runs on now runs the engine it was actually tested with.

Also in this release, quietly: Drive errors say what Google actually objected

access was never granted, sign in again" in one line instead of one day.
