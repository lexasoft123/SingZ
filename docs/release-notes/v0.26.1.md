The reference note has traded its foghorn impression for a piano.

- **Choose your practice sound.** Piano is the new default, with electric piano, nylon guitar and organ available too. Sample levels are balanced, attacks stay natural, and instrument and volume settings are shared across exercises.
- **Make every practice day count.** Multiple sessions on one date count as one practice day. See sessions and exercises for each date, and repeat a lesson in another key that fits your saved range.
- **Follow the exercise more easily.** Clearer instrument and exercise icons, compact sound controls and distinct scale/arpeggio symbols. A successful note hold counts as matched; the progress bar finishes before moving to the next note. Pause stops the exercise correctly.
- **Keep training through supported route changes on iPhone.** Recover to the built-in microphone and speaker when needed, then return to CarPlay when it becomes available. The current exercise restarts; completed exercises remain saved.
- **Fresh listings and screenshots.** Updated desktop, iPhone and Android training images, revised English and Russian descriptions, and new Simplified Chinese store listings.

Songs and project files remain compatible with earlier versions. Store listings and production availability follow their respective publication workflows.
