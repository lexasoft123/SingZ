v0.19.1-mic2 — the microphone stops depending on which phone you own

This is the fix for "the mic does nothing", and the last mic-1 log is what
found it. Thank you for sitting on that note long enough for it to be written
down — the answer was in the numbers, and nothing else could have produced
them.

Two phones, the same log format. A realme's singing VOICE came in at −39 dBFS
— twenty-five decibels below what a Xiaomi's empty ROOM produced sitting idle.
Both phones were working exactly as designed: Android hands a karaoke app the
microphone raw, with no automatic level control (that is what the "live
performance" capture mode means, and it is the right one to ask for), so how
loud raw comes out is entirely the phone's business. SingZ then judged both
against one fixed threshold. The Xiaomi cleared it on 87% of the audio it
heard. The realme cleared it on 0.3%, which is why it only ever twitched when
somebody shouted straight into it.

So SingZ stops assuming. On Android, live capture is now brought up to the
level the pitch detector was built for, per phone, continuously — up to 40 dB
of lift when a phone needs it and none at all when it doesn't. Pitch detection
does not care about level, only about shape, so this changes *whether* your
voice is looked at, never what note it decides you sang. A phone that was
already loud enough takes the untouched path and behaves exactly as before.

Quiet does not mean anything goes. A window still has to be loud enough to
count as somebody singing, on a pair of thresholds set between that realme's
room and that realme's voice — it takes the louder one to start listening and
the quieter one to stop, so a room cannot drift across in a single step.
Without that, a fan or a mains hum lifted forty decibels arrives as a
confident note and can hold a target you never sang.

Also:

- **The log tells the truth about your microphone, not about the lift.** It
  still reports the level your hardware actually produced, so a quiet phone
  stays visibly quiet in the log even while it works fine on screen.
- **"Too quiet — sing louder" is gone on Android**, because it is no longer
  true there and was never advice you could act on. It stays on iPhone, whose
  capture is not lifted, where it still is true. What is new on both is the
  one case singing cannot fix: a microphone delivering nothing at all, which
  now says so and points at Settings.
