v0.19.1 — Re-detect keeps the bar lines you moved

<!-- store:en-US -->
Bar lines you corrected on the computer now survive its Re-detect button. It used to drop them and sync the loss down here, so a song you had already fixed came back wrong on the phone. The phone's own Detect again has always kept them; now both do. Nothing else on the phone changes in this one.

Mac and Windows:

- **Re-detect no longer throws away your bar lines.** If you had nudged a
  couple of bars into the right place on a song and then pressed Re-detect in
  the Metronome window, they went, quietly, and the next save carried the loss
  to Google Drive and on to your phones. It was not the detector disagreeing
  with you — the button simply built the new grid without them. (0.18.0's
  notes warned about this and promised it separately; this is that.)

  Your corrections come back onto the fresh grid now, re-snapped to the beats
  the detector just found. That is what the automatic re-reading has always
  done and what your phone has always done, and one button had no business
  meaning two different things.

  The Metronome window says so, too: beside the grid version it now counts
  what is yours — *· 2 hand-set bars*, in the same green those lines are drawn
  in on the waveform — and the button itself promises to keep them. If you
  want a genuinely clean grid, remove the lines you placed and then press it.

Nothing here changes how a song sounds.
