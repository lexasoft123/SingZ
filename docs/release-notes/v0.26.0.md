Your next interval now has a plan. Your voice can still improvise.

- **A lesson you can follow.** Choose Foundation, Developing, or Advanced, see today's task first, and build a seven-practice-day lesson with saved progress and streaks.
- **Intervals have an order.** Practice six interval sets, three attempts each, from different starting notes. Choose growing intervals or focus on one interval for the week. Four-second reference notes leave more time to hear and settle into the pitch.
- **See what to sing.** Chord notes and whole scales show the current target clearly. Guided scales present each note before your turn. On iPhone, interval names can be spoken before the reference.
- **A clearer desktop workspace.** The training program and progress sit beside the exercise choices in two columns; narrower windows stack them.
- **Feedback stays fair.** A successful hold is scored on the note you settled into. Confirmation tones finish even if your pitch drifts. iPhone secondary-audio hints no longer masquerade as interruptions.
- **A calmer phone library.** The catalog layout stays steady while startup finishes.

Songs and project files remain compatible with earlier versions. iPhone speech announcements are currently specific to iOS.
