v0.26.6 — A little more room to sing

Your iPad has been promoted from a very large phone. The player and library now use the space properly, and a quick tour helps you find the controls before your first chorus.

### Find your way, then find your voice

- **A quick tour of the real controls.** Follow the guide through adding a song, splitting it, mixing the tracks, lyrics and practice. Reopen it with **?** or from Settings whenever you need a reminder.
- **Three more interface languages.** Turkish, Latin American Spanish (including Peru) and Brazilian Portuguese join the language list on desktop and phone. New localized store copy and screenshots accompany them.
- **A clearer look at the loud bits.** Mobile output meters now show held peaks and fading trails, so a brief spike is easier to spot. The bundled sample has more headroom, too.

### iPad and iPhone

- **An iPad-shaped player and library.** Controls, song cards, lyrics and training adapt to wider windows and changing orientations. Gradients fill the whole screen, and wide-screen waveforms are denser without oversized controls.
- **Sheets that know when to leave.** Switching tabs waits for an open sheet to close, avoiding the black screen that could appear on iPad.
- **CarPlay keeps its place.** Audio routes recover their channel mapping, and repeated resume requests no longer restart an already-running song.

### Android

- **A leaner release build.** Code and resources are now shrunk and optimized, with obfuscation enabled and the native audio connections preserved. This addresses the release configuration behind Play's DEX optimization warning; Play will measure the new bundle after upload.

No need to re-split your library. Your songs, settings and practice history stay with you.
