v0.7.0 — your phone joins the band

**The one where your phone joins the band.**

### 📱 SingZ player, now on two phones
- The iPhone player grew up: an Apple-Music-style lyrics mode with the desktop's amber word-by-word glow, a real project catalog, and vocal training on the go.
- **Android is here.** Grab the APK below, point it at your projects folder, sing. Six stems, karaoke, training — the whole thing.
- Phones now measure your actual audio path (Bluetooth and fancy speaker DSPs add lag — we probe it with a timestamp trick instead of guessing), so words light up when the sound reaches your ears.
- Survived its own success: big songs used to pile up gigabytes until the phone quietly executed the app mid-chorus. Buffers are shared now, seeks coalesce, and the fifth song opens as calmly as the first.

### 🎚 Key & speed, for real
- Transpose and tempo on phones now bend actual audio (Signalsmith Stretch on the master bus). Take that impossible song down two semitones and 10% slower while you learn it — the pitch stays where you put it.

### ☁️ Your library follows you
- **Google Drive sync, without installing Google Drive.** The desktop pushes projects to a visible SingZ folder in your Drive — automatically after every save, only what changed. Phones sign in once and stream stems straight from the cloud. The Android sign-in even cleans up its own browser tab, and shrugs off MIUI's enthusiasm for killing background network.
- Projects are ~4× smaller: stems are lossless FLAC now. Old projects upgrade themselves the first time you open them.

### 🖥 Desktop
- The project explorer is a proper card grid — search, your folder's cloud status in plain words, and a little Drive badge on each project telling you if it's synced, syncing, or sulking.
- Leaving training mode mid-song no longer holds your vocals hostage, and the count-in dots finally emigrated to phones.

### 📦 The fine print
- The bundled demo song is now "Sing with me" — an original, written and synthesized specifically for SingZ. Tennessee Ernie Ford sends his regards, and takes 36 MB of app size with him.
- The app is called SingZ everywhere now, including your phone's home screen.
- Android: sideload `SingZ-v0.7.0-android.apk` below. Mac and Windows: install once, auto-update forever.
