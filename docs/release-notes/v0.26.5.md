v0.26.5 — Every song, at your level

Quiet song? Turn up that song, not the whole evening. SingZ now remembers an output level for each song, with up to +12 dB of boost and a peak meter that warns when the signal clips.

### Desktop and phone

- **A level for every song.** Adjust Song output, watch the peak meter, and reset to the original level whenever you like. The setting is saved with the project and follows it between desktop and phone through Drive.
- **Nine more interface languages.** Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Filipino, Malay and Traditional Chinese join English, Russian and Simplified Chinese. Longer labels fit more comfortably, player controls stay reachable in narrow windows, and the language menu scrolls on short screens.

### Singing practice on your phone

- **Your song comes first.** Preparation for the selected song appears before the daily routine. Review the key, range and exercise settings before starting.
- **Finish without losing your place.** Ending a session returns to training instead of unexpectedly jumping back to the song.
- **Settings where you expect them.** Songs and Training share the same settings screen. Audio diagnostics live inside Settings, and the training log is one tap away in the header.
- **An optional iPhone analysis mode.** Extended pitch analysis is now off by default and can be enabled in Audio diagnostics. It is an experimental option, not a promise of better results in every room.

### A calmer Drive library

- **On your phone:** returning to SingZ refreshes the visible Drive library quietly. It also checks periodically while you browse, without replacing your songs with a loading screen.
- **On your computer:** downloads show the current file, bytes and speed. Temporary failures retry automatically. Cancelling keeps completed, verified files, so the next sync can continue without downloading them again. Incomplete files never replace verified copies.
