v0.19.1-mic3 — the threshold stops sitting inside your voice

mic-2 could hear you. It just couldn't do it every time, and your log says
exactly why in one line: a session that came back with nothing recorded
"lift up to 1.0x", meaning the microphone was open, audio was arriving, and
SingZ decided none of it was loud enough to be a person singing.

That session peaked at −50.1 dBFS. The level SingZ required was −49.9. Two
tenths of a decibel — and across the eight sessions in your log, the sessions
where you WERE heard peaked anywhere between −43.0 and −48.4. So the bar was
set inside the range of your own voice, and which side of it you landed on was
close to chance. That is what "works but not stable" is.

The bar is now −55 dBFS, comfortably under every session in that log,
including the one that failed. Nothing else about how the sound is handled has
changed.

This is a real trade, so here is the straight version. On this phone your
voice and your room are only a few decibels apart, so a bar low enough to
always catch you is also low enough to let a steady mechanical hum — a fan, a
fridge, mains buzz — through to the note detector. Nothing downstream filters
that out: a hum is a steady tone, and steadiness is exactly what the detector
is looking for. So a loud enough hum can put a note on the meter while nobody
is singing, and if it happens to land on the note you were asked for, it can
run out the hold on its own.

If you see that, please say so — it is the other end of this trade and the fix
for it is a good one (the app should not accept a note that was already
sounding before it asked you to sing). What it is not is a reason to put the
bar back up, which is what made it deaf to you in the first place.
