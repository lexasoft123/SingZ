v0.14.4 — SingZ is on Google Play, and it was never silent on purpose

The Android app is on the Play Store now, in closed testing, which means it
arrives on a phone by itself and updates the same way. No more sideloading an
APK from a link and telling people to trust it. It also means the first round
of testers found something in an afternoon that we had not found in months.

## "It doesn't play songs" — it did, the whole time

Twelve people got the app. Several reported it would not play. It was playing
perfectly, at zero volume, on phones whose media volume happened to sit at the
bottom — and here is the cruel part: pressing volume-up did not help either,
because Android only points those keys at the media stream once sound is
already audibly coming out. So the app was silent, the obvious fix did nothing,
and the only reasonable conclusion was that it was broken.

The volume keys now mean *media* from the moment SingZ opens, playing or not.
One line of code, one afternoon of everyone's time.

## The log stopped going quiet exactly when it mattered

The old log described every byte fetched from Google Drive in loving detail and
then said nothing whatsoever about what happened next. Diagnosing the silence
above came down to asking a human to look at a number on screen and say whether
it was moving.

Now the log opens with which build, on which phone, on which Android, and how
much memory is free — the header nobody thinks to include in a bug report.
Opening a song records the formats going in, and then the wall time, the decoded
megabytes and the decode time of every stem. *Wish You Were Here* reads
"6 lanes from the folder in 3.1 s · 783 MB decoded", which is also a quiet
explanation of why long songs are hard work for a phone.

And starting a song writes down where the sound is going and how loud. If the
media volume is zero, it says so in red, in words, and tells you what to press.

## Google Drive says what it actually objected to

A refusal from Drive used to show a number and nothing else. It now carries
Google's own reason — and for the one cause you can actually do something about,
where sign-in completed but the Drive tick box was never granted, it says so and
tells you to sign in again. Signing in also records which permissions Google
handed over, because that consent screen is easier to half-approve than anyone
expects.

## Магазин теперь говорит по-русски

The Play listing is in Russian as well as English: title, description, and the
release notes you are reading a translation of. If your phone is Russian, the
store page now is too.

---

Nothing changed about the singing. Six tracks, word-by-word lyrics, the pitch
line, transpose, slow-down, loops and training mode all behave exactly as they
did in 0.14.0 — this release is about the app being reachable, and about it
being able to tell you what is wrong instead of leaving you to guess.
