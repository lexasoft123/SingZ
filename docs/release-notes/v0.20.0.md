Mac and Windows:

- **A lyrics editor.** SingZ has always guessed at lyrics — a database match,
  or its own ear on the vocals when nothing matched. Guesses are sometimes
  wrong, and until now there was nothing to do about it but live with the
  wrong words or give up on karaoke for that song. Press **Edit** in the
  lyrics panel and type or paste the real thing.

  Every line carries a small picture of the vocals underneath it, so a line
  that's actually silence — a stretch the listening software imagined words
  into — shows itself before you even read it, and one button clears every
  line like that at once. Click a line's picture and it opens into its
  separate words, each one a chip sitting where it's sung; drag one to
  retime it, or set it exactly on the beat with a double-click while the
  song plays. **Align to the singing** does the same job for the whole
  song in one pass, and says honestly what it found — which lines it
  couldn't quite make out rather than pretending everything matched. A
  **?** in the corner explains every key and gesture, on both platforms'
  own keyboards.

  Fix it once here, and every phone signed into the same library gets the
  correction the next time it syncs — nothing to redo on the small screen.

Nothing here changes how a song sounds.
