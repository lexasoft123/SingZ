v0.20.1 — the editor grows a line, instead of splitting one backward

<!-- store:en-US -->
Nothing on your phone changed in this one. It fixes the lyrics editor, a Mac/Windows feature: on the computer, adding a line to your lyrics could split it the wrong way round instead, and now there's a small + on every line that just adds a line. None of that touches your phone.

Mac and Windows:

- **Adding a line now does what it says.** The lyrics editor's Enter key
  splits the text you're already typing at wherever the cursor happens to
  be — that's how it retimes a run-on line into two. It was also the only
  way to add a line at all, and clicking anywhere but the very last
  character split it backward: an empty row where you clicked, your real
  words pushed down after it. If a whole verse or chorus was missing —
  nothing whatsoever transcribed for it — there was no line to click into
  in the first place, and no way in.

  Every line now carries a small **+** next to the line-remove button. It
  adds an empty line right after the one you're on and puts your cursor
  in it — no clicking at exactly the right spot required. Type the missing
  words, then stamp them with the playhead (⌘Enter / Ctrl+Enter) while the
  song plays past that point, the same as any other line. Press **+** again
  from the new line to keep going — a whole missing section fills in one
  line at a time.

iPhone and Android: nothing changes here — the editor is a Mac/Windows
feature, and this release carries no other change on either phone.

What actually happened, since Enter's old behaviour wasn't wrong on its own
terms: splitting a line at the cursor is exactly what you want when you're
breaking one run-on line into two. The bug was that it was also the *only*
way in, so a keystroke meant for retiming text you already had was standing
in for "add a new line," a job it was never shaped for. The fix gives
"add a new line" its own control instead of asking Enter to guess which job
you meant.
