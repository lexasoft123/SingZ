v0.16.1 — the phone stops splitting in secret

<!-- store:en-US -->
Splitting a song on Android now shows up where you would expect it: a notification with a progress bar and a Cancel button, so you can leave the app and still see how far it has got. And when the phone refuses to start a split at all — screen off, battery Restricted — it says so, with the phone's own reason, instead of quietly forgetting you asked.

Android:

- **You can watch the split now.** Tap Split and the phone asks whether it may
  show notifications. Say yes and you get a "Splitting into stems"
  notification with a progress bar and a Cancel button — so you can leave the
  app, go and do something else, and still know where it got to, and it never
  asks again. Say no and the split runs exactly the same; you just only see
  progress while you are on that screen. Android gives it one more try on your
  next split and then stops asking — if you change your mind after that,
  notifications for SingZ are still there in Android's own settings.

  This is why 0.16.0 could look like it had done nothing. Android 13 and
  newer suppress notifications until an app asks for permission, and this one
  never asked. The notification was built, the Cancel button was on it, and
  neither ever reached the screen — so leaving the app during a split meant
  the work carried on invisibly, with no way to stop it.

- **A split that cannot start now says so.** Android sometimes refuses to
  begin one — the screen went off at the wrong moment, or the battery setting
  for the app is "Restricted". The card used to sit on "Starting…" and then
  quietly clear, which looked exactly like nothing had happened, because
  nothing had. It now tells you the split could not start, suggests keeping
  the screen on, and passes along the phone's own reason. If there was
  already progress, Resume still picks up where it stopped.

- **And the case where the phone never even tries.** On some phones the
  system drops the request without a word. That now reads "The split never
  started — try again" rather than a card that empties itself.

iPhone, Mac and Windows: nothing changes for you in this one — it exists
because Android had two ways of doing work you could not see. Your computer
will still fetch 0.16.1 — the Mac offers it, Windows just takes it on the next
restart — and either is fine; on your side it is the same app.
