# SingZ is now publicly available on Google Play

The public store listing was verified on 24 October 2026. This announces Android production availability of v0.26.4, rather than a new app version.

## English caption

SingZ v0.26.4 🎤

SingZ is now publicly available on Google Play!

Bring your own song, remove the lead vocal and sing with synced lyrics. Change the key, loop a difficult passage, or practise your pitch with guided vocal exercises — right on your Android phone.

No tester invitation needed. Install from Google Play and try the included sample song.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lexasoft.singz
