v0.9.0 — the band counts you in

### Desktop

- **Metronome, with an optional count-in.** SingZ now reads the beat from the drums stem — the *real* beat, not a guess: it follows the tempo drift of recordings made before click tracks existed. Arm a one- or two-bar count-in and press play: clicks at the song's own tempo, amber dots filling by the clock, and the band enters on the downbeat. Songs that breathe too freely for any metronome (hello, *Music of the Night*) are politely refused a click — you can still tap your own.
- The click follows your speed changes and ignores your transpose, as a good drummer would. Tempo taps, nudges, accents and the count-in setting all save with the project.
- **"Use AI transcription instead" now visibly does something.** It always worked — behind the search list, where nobody could watch. It's a chip at the top of the search now, and clicking it shows the listening progress.
- **Projects from anywhere.** A project folder someone copied or shared to you — outside your library — now opens, plays and saves in place, with an add-to-library action when you want it home.
- Saving while signed out of Google Drive now says so, instead of silently skipping the sync.

### iPhone & Android

- **The metronome came along.** Projects saved by this desktop version carry their beat track; the phones click along it, count you in with dots above the scrubber, and hold still until the downbeat. Older projects show a gentle hint to re-save on desktop.
- **Your library appears instantly when you leave a song** — the Drive refresh happens behind the cached list instead of in front of your face. Pull-to-refresh now features exactly one spinner.
- A back-to-start button, because scrubbing to 0:00 with a thumb was never music.

### Under the hood

- Repeat native builds are cached with ccache on dev machines (whisper vendor build: 31 s cold → 7 s warm). CI already knew this trick; now your laptop does too.
- Installers and splitter packs attach below as the builds finish — sizes unchanged from 0.8.x.
