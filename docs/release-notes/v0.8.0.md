v0.8.0 — the words learn to tell the truth

**The one where the words learn to tell the truth.**

### 🎤 Lyrics that check themselves
- **Check & align**: SingZ now listens to the vocals and compares them with the downloaded lyrics, word by word. You get an honest verdict — *matches*, *re-timed to this recording*, or *"these lyrics don't seem to match this recording"* (wrong-version lyrics can no longer silently ruin your karaoke night). Timing snaps to what's actually sung; lines the singer never sings as written are flagged instead of guessed.
- **Precise**: for the sharpest timing, a multilingual word aligner (1,100+ languages, one-time ~1.2 GB download) pins every word to the exact moment it leaves the singer's mouth — including "Here I am … *[two bars of nothing]* … on the road again". Works on Apple Silicon, Windows **and** Intel Macs through the splitter pack you already have.
- Already caught in the wild: one song whose database timing came from an edit with a shorter guitar solo (everything 6 seconds early), and one whose chorus line was stamped 45 seconds late. Both fixed by pressing one button.
- Karaoke now **leads like karaoke should**: words light up a breath before they're sung, and the first line of every song gets a proper count-in, even on quick starts.
- Both models are optional downloads in the model manager, and the buttons ask before downloading anything.

### 📱 Phones
- **Your phone stopped eating songs.** Closing a song now returns its memory immediately (a 4-minute song is ~700 MB of decoded audio; five opens used to be a silent crash on iPhone). Scrub, close, reopen all night.
- The Drive library lists **5× faster**, shows a real loading indicator instead of impersonating a frozen app, and coming back from a song is instant and offline — the list is cached, and stems were always cached after first play.
- The screen stays awake while SingZ is open. Music stands don't have thumbs.
- Renaming a project on the desktop no longer leaves its ghost haunting your phone's Drive list (old folders go to Drive's trash, recoverable for 30 days).
- Re-timed lyrics sync to your phone automatically after aligning — no ritual re-saving.

### 🖥 Desktop
- Suggested project names keep their dots: "Mr. Crowley" is no longer filed under "Mr".
- The splitter pack got pinned and smoke-tested at build time, so a future dependency update can't quietly break splitting.

### 📦 The fine print
- Android: sideload `SingZ-v0.8.0-android.apk` below. Mac and Windows auto-update as usual.
- The whisper speech model (~1.6 GB) does the checking; on Windows it thinks hard (CPU) — the progress bar is honest. Precise alignment takes ~1–3 minutes per song depending on the machine.
