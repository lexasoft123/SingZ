v0.19.1-mic1 — a test build that writes down what the microphone is doing

**This is a test build for one report, not a release.** It is a prerelease, so
it will never show up as "latest" and no updater will offer it. Nothing about
your songs changes. Train now keeps notes on what the microphone is doing, and
two things about it actually behave differently — both at the bottom.

If you hit "the microphone does nothing" — the note is showing, the transport
says LISTENING, and nothing ever appears under "You are singing":

1. Let it sit on that note for about ten seconds rather than skipping straight
   past it. The build needs those seconds to notice and write down what it is
   seeing.
2. Go back to Songs, tap **Log** in the top right, then **Share**, and send it
   over.

That log now answers the question the old one could not. Before this build the
microphone wrote nothing at all, so a report of "it doesn't work" and a report
of "I sang too quietly" arrived looking identical — and on a phone there is no
other way to look.

What it records, per attempt: every audio input Android offered and which one
was picked, the rate, lane and capture mode that were negotiated, how long the
first audio took to arrive, how loud it was, and — if nothing arrives at all —
a line saying so, with the counters from inside the audio engine that say
whether the microphone hardware ever called back.

Two things also changed that you may actually notice:

- **The screen stops saying "Waiting for your voice" when it isn't waiting.**
  It now tells apart the two cases it used to blur together: it can hear
  you but you are too quiet to read, or no sound is arriving at all. In the
  second case it points at Replay, which is what genuinely brings capture back.
- **Connected earbuds no longer steal the microphone.** Bluetooth ranked above
  the phone's own microphone when choosing where to listen, while SingZ never
  asks Android to route capture over Bluetooth — so a pair of earbuds you had
  connected for listening could aim it at an input it was never going to hear.
