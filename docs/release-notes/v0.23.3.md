v0.23.3 — the laptop wakes up, and so does the music

<!-- store:en-US -->
MP3 songs you haven't split yet — and MP3 tracks you've added to a song — now play on SingZ's own audio engine, read a little at a time as they play instead of being unpacked whole into memory first. A six-minute MP3 used to cost about 140 MB of memory just to open. On the computer, a Windows laptop that slept with a song paused, or lost its audio device another way, no longer leaves every song silent until you restart SingZ.

MP3-песни, которые вы ещё не разделили, и MP3-дорожки, добавленные к песне, теперь играют на собственном звуковом движке SingZ и читаются понемногу по ходу песни, а не распаковываются целиком в память заранее. Раньше шестиминутная MP3 только на открытие съедала около 140 МБ памяти. На компьютере: ноутбук с Windows, уснувший с песней на паузе или как-то иначе потерявший звуковое устройство, больше не оставляет все песни без звука до перезапуска SingZ.
<!-- /store -->

Windows:

- **A nap no longer silences SingZ until you restart it.** Pause a song,
  let the laptop sleep, come back — and Windows has quietly swapped the
  audio device out from under the paused song. SingZ then refused to let
  go of that song: adding a track, opening another song or pressing Play
  did nothing, however many times you pressed it, until you closed and
  reopened the app. Losing the audio device any other way — a USB or
  Bluetooth headset unplugged, speakers switched off in Windows — could
  do the same. Now the old song lets go, and the next Play
  starts on whatever output Windows has now.

Everywhere:

- **MP3s play without unpacking the whole song first.** An MP3 you haven't
  split yet, or an MP3 you added to a song as your own track, used to be
  decoded in full before the first note — about 140 MB of memory for a
  six-minute song, on every open. SingZ now reads MP3s the way it already
  read its FLAC stems: a little at a time, as the song plays, with seeks
  that land on the exact sample.
