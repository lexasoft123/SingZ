v0.22.0 — the band plays on when you lock your phone, and the computer counts you in every time

<!-- store:en-US -->
Lock your phone and the band plays on. The song you are singing now shows on the lock screen with play, pause, a scrub bar and ten-second skips, so the phone can go in your pocket on the way to the mic and you can still stop the track without unlocking it. On the computer, the count-in clicks before every Play instead of only the first, and songs open about five times faster.

Nothing here touches a song you already have: no grid re-derives, no melody
line re-tracks, no lyrics move.

iPhone and Android:

- **Your song is on the lock screen.** Press the side button mid-chorus and
  the song keeps going — and now you can see it doing so. The lock screen,
  Control Center on iPhone and the media notification on Android all show
  the song with its title, where you are in it, play and pause, a bar you
  can drag, and ten seconds back or forward. The phone goes in your pocket,
  you walk to the mic, and the track still stops when you tell it to.
  Counting in holds the timeline still until the song lands, and a slowed
  song's timeline runs at the slowed speed, so the lock screen never gets
  ahead of the band.

- **Android keeps playing when you leave the app.** Until now a song on
  Android paused the moment you went to the home screen or locked the phone,
  because nothing had asked the system to keep it alive. A playing song now
  holds a media notification that does exactly that, and lets go of that hold
  the moment you pause — the notification stays, and can be swiped away — so a
  paused song costs your battery nothing. Swipe SingZ
  away from your recent apps and the song pauses instead of playing on with
  nowhere to stop it. If Android refuses the request — it does from the
  background in some cases — the song pauses on leaving the app, as before.

Mac and Windows:

- **The count-in counts in every time.** In 0.21.1 the count-in sounded once
  per song and never again — not after you scrubbed somewhere, not after a
  pause. Every Play with the count-in on clicks you in now, from wherever
  you are, and a pause in the middle of the count-in counts in again to the
  same spot. The dots light with each click again, too.

- **The seek bar stops jumping to the start.** A count-in from the middle of
  a song drew the seek bar at the very top of the song for as long as it
  clicked, and pausing during it left the bar there. The bar now waits at
  the spot you picked while the clicks run. Turning the click on or off
  mid-count-in no longer brings the song in at the top either, and pressing
  Space several times fast no longer flicks the bar to the top of the song
  for an instant — for a moment after every play or pause the engine had not
  yet worked out where the song was being heard, and the bar drew that as
  the very beginning.

- **Songs open about five times faster.** Opening a song used to decode
  every lane into memory before it appeared — a few seconds for a long song
  on a MacBook, much longer on an older laptop — only for the player to read
  the same files again itself. The lanes are measured straight off their
  files now: a five-minute, six-lane song opens in about half a second
  instead of 2.7, and the decoded copy — about 740 MB for that song — is
  simply never made.

Why the lock screen, and why now: the iPhone app's first App Store review
came back with one note — SingZ says it plays audio in the background, and
the reviewer could not see anything doing it. The song was playing all
along; nothing outside the app said so. Now something does, on both phones.
